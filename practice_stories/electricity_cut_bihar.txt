Practice case (not a real person).
Speaker: a woman aged 40 who lives in a village near Gaya, Bihar, with her two children.
Her electricity connection was cut on 1 October 2026, about five weeks ago, although she has paid every bill.
She has the latest bill and the receipts of the last three payments. She was not given any notice.
She went to the local electricity office twice. The lineman said the line would be restored
only if she paid "kharcha-pani" of Rs 2,000.
Her family earns about Rs 6,000 a month. The children cannot study at night.
She wants the connection restored and no extra payment.
No one is in danger and no case has been filed.
