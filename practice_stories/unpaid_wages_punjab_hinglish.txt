Practice case (koi asli vyakti nahi).
Bolne wala: ek factory mazdoor ka bhai. Mazdoor Ludhiana, Punjab mein ek cycle parts factory mein kaam karta tha.
Malik ne 30 September ke baad se salary nahi di. Har mahine 12000 rupaye milne the, teen mahine se kuch nahi mila.
Malik kehta hai "order aayega tab denge". Ab factory ne usse kaam se bhi nikal diya.
Unke paas attendance card ki photo hai. Woh chahte hain ki bakaya tankhwah mil jaye.
Koi khatra nahi hai aur abhi tak koi complaint nahi ki.
