### When to use which
- Someone is in danger right now: 112 first, always.
- Money lost to online fraud: 1930 immediately, then the bank in writing.
- Needs a free lawyer for a court case: 15100 or the District Legal Services Authority.
- Needs advice before any case is filed: Tele-Law 14454.
- Woman facing violence at home: 181, and the nearest One Stop Centre.
- A child at risk: 1098.
