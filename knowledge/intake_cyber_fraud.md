# Intake checklist: cyber and financial fraud

Ask one question at a time, in this order. Stop and give the urgent steps as soon as question 2 shows money was lost in the last few days. Never record names, phone numbers, account numbers in full, Aadhaar or addresses: keep only the last 4 digits of any account or card.

## Urgent check (ask first)
1. Is anyone being threatened or in danger right now? [Yes / No]
   - If yes: tell them to call 112 now.
2. When did the money leave the account? [In the last few hours / Today / 1–3 days ago / 4–7 days ago / More than 7 days ago / No money lost yet]
   - If within a few days and 1930 not yet called: tell them to call 1930 now, before the rest of the intake.

## What happened
3. What kind of fraud was it? [UPI or payment app / Card / Net banking / Fake investment or job / "Digital arrest" or fake police call / Loan app / Other]
4. Has 1930 been called or a complaint filed on cybercrime.gov.in? [Yes, with acknowledgement number / Yes, no number / No]
5. Has the bank been told in writing (email or letter)? [Yes, date: ___ / Only by phone / No]
6. How much money was lost in total? [amount in ₹]
7. How many transactions? For each: date, time, amount, transaction ID or UTR. [list]
8. Did the person share an OTP, PIN, password or install an app the caller asked for? [Yes / No / Not sure]
   - This decides how the RBI liability rules apply (see cyber_fraud.md).
9. When did the person get the bank's SMS or email about the transaction? [date]
   - Needed to count the 3 and 7 working-day windows (for transactions from 1 January 2027: the 5 calendar-day window, counted from the transaction).
10. What details of the fraudster do they have? [phone numbers / UPI IDs / account numbers / website or app name / none]
11. What evidence have they kept? [SMS alerts / screenshots / call logs / chat / none]

## Eligibility for free legal aid (only if a lawyer may be needed)
12. State where the person lives. [state]
13. Do any of these apply: woman, child, SC/ST, person with disability, senior citizen (some states), industrial worker? [list]
14. Annual family income, roughly. [₹]

## Facts needed before drafts
- For the cyber complaint: items 3, 6, 7, 10, 11
- For the bank letter: items 6, 7, 9 and the 1930 acknowledgement number
- For a legal aid application: items 12–14 and a short statement of what happened
