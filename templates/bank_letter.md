Notes for the volunteer
- Send within 3 working days of the bank's alert if at all possible (5 calendar days of the transaction for transactions from 1 January 2027): this decides how much the customer can lose under the RBI rules (see cyber_fraud.md).
- For credit card fraud on or after 1 January 2027, the bank must give a temporary credit within 5 calendar days of the report.
- Send by email to the branch's official address and customer care, and keep the sent email as proof.
- If the bank doesn't reply within 30 days or the customer is unhappy with the reply, they can complain to the RBI Ombudsman at cms.rbi.org.in within the next 90 days.
- Sources: https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=13140 (rules until 31 December 2026); https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13543 (rules from 1 January 2027)
