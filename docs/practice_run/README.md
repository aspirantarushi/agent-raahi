# Practice Clinic run: coordinator's guide

A practice run of Agent Raahi with five volunteers and one supervisor, on the live app, with made-up
cases only. Nothing here is a real person or a real case.

- Everyone signs in with their **label exactly as written** ("Volunteer A", "Supervisor"), never their own name.
- The supervisor is played by someone who is **not** one of the five volunteers (no one can approve their own work).
- The stories are in [stories.md](stories.md). Hand each person their sheet and the stories file.
- Afterwards, everyone fills in [feedback_form.md](feedback_form.md) (no personal details).

## Before the run

1. Give the volunteers the clinic passcode, and the supervisor the supervisor password, by word of mouth
   (never in these files).
2. Check the app opens on every phone or laptop being used.
3. Keep a clock or phone timer handy: each sheet asks for the minutes each case took.

## The camp

Cases 6, 11 and 13 are taken at a camp. Each volunteer with a camp case sets the camp before starting it:
**Village or place:** `Practice camp` · **District:** `Gaya district (Bihar)` · **Date:** the day of the run.
(The app shows it as "Practice camp, Gaya district (Bihar)".)

## Order of play

1. All five volunteers work through their sheets at the same time.
2. Volunteer A gives Volunteer B the **code from the case slip** of case 11 (on paper, or read out).
3. The supervisor reviews cases as they arrive (they show as "Waiting for review").
4. When the supervisor approves case 1 and case 4, they tell Volunteer A and Volunteer D, who then record
   the submission and the acknowledgement in Case tracker.
5. At the end: collect the sheets (with the minutes) and the feedback forms.

## How to do a case (the same for every volunteer)

**Start a case**
1. Press **Start a case** (top right; on a phone, open the **Menu**).
2. Leave **Any problem** chosen, except for the undertrial stories (1, 2, 3): choose **Section 479: undertrial**.
3. Copy the story from stories.md into the story box exactly (undertrial stories: see the end of this section). Check the **Language of the notes** (choose
   हिंदी for story 9 if it is not chosen already).
4. Press **Read the story** and wait (it can take about 30 seconds).

**Questions**
5. Answer each question from the story's **Answers if asked** list and press **Next**. If the question is
   not on the list, press **Don't know**.

**Draft and research (step 3)**
6. If the sheet says **run research**: choose the state if asked, press **Run research**, read the points,
   leave them all ticked, then press **Draft the letter**.
7. In the readiness checklist, tick what is true. For anything you can't tick, write a short reason in the
   box "Why are you sending it anyway?", for example `practice run: not known`.
8. Press **Make the drafts**.

**Send (step 4)**
9. Tick **I have checked this summary against the notes, and it contains no names of people**.
10. Press **Send to supervisor**. You should see "Sent on … · Waiting for review".
11. Press **Finish intake**.

For the undertrial stories (1, 2, 3) the screen is slightly different:
1. Open **Paste case notes (optional): the AI fills in the form for you**, paste the story, and press **Read notes**.
2. Answer the questions from the story's list (**Don't know** for anything else).
3. Check every field in the form against the story, and tick **I have checked every field against the notes**.
4. Press **Calculate the dates**, answer the lawyer question, then press **Make draft**.
5. Press **Send to supervisor**.

**Start only** means: do steps 1–5 (read the story and answer the questions), then stop. Don't send.

**Record the submission and acknowledgement** (cases 1 and 4, after approval): open **Case tracker**, find
the case by its code, open it, and in **Handoff**:
- **Record the submission**: Authorised worker `Clinic coordinator (practice)`, today's date, How `in person`.
- **Record the acknowledgement**: Acknowledged by `DLSA`, reference `PRACTICE-1` (case 1) or `PRACTICE-4`
  (case 4), Recorded by your label.
