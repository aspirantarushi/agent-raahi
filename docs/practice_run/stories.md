# Practice Clinic: the 15 stories

Every story is made up. No real person, no real case. People are described by their role ("the
elder sister", "the contractor"), never by name. Copy a story exactly into the story box, as written.

Each story lists **Answers if asked**. When the app asks something that is in that list, give that
answer. When it asks anything else, press **Don't know**.

---

## Story 1 · Undertrial, Section 479 date reached · Bihar · Volunteer A

> The elder sister of an undertrial prisoner came to the clinic. Her younger brother, aged 27, was arrested on 10 January 2024 by the police in Gaya district, Bihar, for a theft from a house in their village on 15 December 2023. He is charged only under IPC 380 (theft in a dwelling house). He has never been convicted before and there is no other case against him. He has been in Gaya jail since the arrest. He has a legal aid lawyer whom he has met twice. Bail was never applied for. The family wants him released.

Answers if asked:
- Date custody began: 10 January 2024
- Date of the offence: 15 December 2023
- Section: IPC 380, only one section
- Ever convicted before: Never
- Other cases pending: No
- Delay caused by him: No
- Lawyer: Legal aid lawyer

What the app should show (for the supervisor): IPC 380 = BNS 305, maximum 84 months. One-third (28 months):
10 May 2026, already reached. One-half (42 months): 10 July 2027. Release is not automatic: the jail
superintendent must apply and the court decides.

## Story 2 · Undertrial, life-sentence offence · Uttar Pradesh · Volunteer B

> A mother from Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, says her son, aged 22, was arrested on 3 March 2025 after a fight in the market on 1 March 2025 in which a man died. He is charged with murder under BNS 103(1). He has never been convicted before and there is no other case. He has no lawyer. The family earns about Rs 90,000 a year. She wants to know what can be done for him.

Answers if asked:
- Date custody began: 3 March 2025
- Date of the offence: 1 March 2025
- Section: BNS 103(1), only one section
- Ever convicted before: Never
- Other cases pending: No
- Delay caused by him: No
- Lawyer: None
- Family income: Rs 90,000 a year

What the app should show: murder can be punished with death or life imprisonment, so Section 479 does
not apply. He qualifies for free legal aid (in custody), so a DLSA application is drafted.

> मेरी बड़ी बहन, उम्र 32 साल, जयपुर, राजस्थान में अपने ससुराल में रहती थी। शादी के बाद से उसका पति उसके साथ मारपीट करता है। 28 अक्टूबर 2026 को पति ने उसे और उसके दो बच्चों को घर से निकाल दिया। अभी वह हमारे मायके में सुरक्षित है। उसके पास अस्पताल की एक पर्ची है। वह चाहती है कि उसे और बच्चों को ख़र्चा मिले और उसे ससुराल के घर में रहने का हक़ मिले।

Choose **हिंदी** as the language of the notes if the app does not suggest it.

- वह क्या चाहती है (What she wants): ख़र्चा और घर में रहने का हक़ (maintenance, and the right to live in the shared home)
