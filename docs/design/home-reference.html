How a case moves

From a story to the right door

01

Tell the story

Type, paste or speak it, in English or Hindi. Phone and Aadhaar numbers are removed first.

02

Answer a few questions

Only what is missing, one at a time. "Don't know" is always fine; nothing is guessed.

03

Check the summary

What happened, urgency, free legal aid, documents and next steps, with a ready-for-review checklist.

04

A lawyer approves

The supervisor approves or sends it back. Then it is submitted, acknowledged and followed up.

Built to be safe

It helps the volunteer. It never replaces the lawyer.

A lawyer approves every draft

Nothing is saved or sent until a supervisor approves, and no one approves their own work.

A source behind every legal point

Law comes only from checked files, each with its source. Anything else says "ask a lawyer".

No names or Aadhaar stored

Personal numbers are removed before the AI sees anything, and notes are never saved.

Says "cannot conclude yet"

When facts contradict or are missing, it stops and asks instead of guessing.

Never predicts outcomes

No bail chances, risk scores or promises. Release is never automatic.

Works without AI too

Dates and eligibility are plain, tested rules. If the AI is down, the form still works.
